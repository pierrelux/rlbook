When to close a storm-surge barrier

A storm is forecast to push the water at Rotterdam to a peak in 24 hours. Every hour the forecast of that peak is updated and becomes more accurate. Ordering the closure halts shipping at once, and the gates need at least 6 hours to close, sometimes more. If the barrier is still open when the peak exceeds 300 cm above NAP, the city floods.
