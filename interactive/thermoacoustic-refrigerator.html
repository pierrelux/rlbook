Synthetic lumped teaching model with illustrative coefficients, not a measured device. The acoustic cycle is a cartoon at 1.2 Hz with an exaggerated stroke, and the piston is drawn quasi-statically, pushed in when the driver-end pressure peaks; a real device runs at hundreds of hertz, and at resonance it is the piston's velocity, not its position, that is in phase with the pressure. Plans are recorded iLQR/DDP solutions replayed as fixed open-loop sequences; scrubbing does not replan. The constant-amplitude plan is integrated in the page with the same RK4 step and checked at load against the recorded Python rollout. Because the model is deterministic and the plan is fixed, the terminal cost is known before playback. Time is counted in steps from t = 0 (the initial state) to t = 300 (the final state, one second per step); the chapter indexes the same plan from 1 to T = 301.
