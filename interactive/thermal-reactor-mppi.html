Model, uncertainty, and recorded computation

This is an illustrative 8 m × 2 m flow-through reactor, discretized into 64 × 32 cells. Each cell stores temperature and remaining reactant fraction. Flow moves left to right at 0.2 m/s. Conversion is one minus the reactant fraction.

Four heating fractions lie between zero and one. The target is at least 95% outlet conversion while keeping all cells at or below 1070 K. The observed inlet fluctuation has a 20 s correlation time and a 15 K stationary standard deviation.

The controller replans every 2 s over 40 s. Its default update uses 128 candidate sequences, four conditional inlet futures, and two weighted updates. Proposed controls are checked with the PDE under conditional mean inlet conditions. This check does not guarantee feasibility under every random disturbance.

The surrogate projects predicted reactant fractions onto [0, 1]; its temperature predictions are unrestricted. This prevents impossible fractions but does not establish prediction accuracy. The validation report includes the raw fraction-bound excess.
