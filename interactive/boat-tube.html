Pushing the boat off its plan

Twelve copies of the boat follow the final iLQR docking plan and each receives a different push at 2 s. On the left they replay the planned thrusts; on the right they apply the local feedback u = ūt + Kt(x − x̄t). The shaded band is the tube traced by the feedback runs after the small pushes.
