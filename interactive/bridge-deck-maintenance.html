Maintaining bridge decks for the long run

Each bridge's deck is colored by its condition rating, from 9 (new) to 4 or below (poor). Every year a deck may get worse, following transition rates counted from US National Bridge Inventory records. The top group of 12 decks is managed by the optimal policy from value and policy iteration, the bottom group by a common rule of thumb. Both groups start in the same state and draw the same random numbers, so any difference comes from the decisions. A crew marker shows the work done that year.

Do-nothing transitions are counted from annual NBI deck ratings (item 58) of 88,596 decks whose rating never went up, 1993 to 2019, as mirrored in the MUSTEM repository of Liu, Nehme and Lu. Action costs (USD per m²) and failure probabilities by rating follow Saifullah et al.; the effect of each repair on the rating is a teaching assumption. Costs are discounted at 3% a year.
